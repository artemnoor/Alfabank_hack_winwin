## What is actually reusable

The reviewed vendors demonstrate market use of multi-tier relationship maps, explicit entity/relationship types, provenance and freshness metadata, AI-suggested links with verification, and supplier collaboration. This supports testing those **general design ideas** in T12; it does not establish that any particular vendor's private algorithm, ontology, data, score, coverage, or accuracy is available to us.

No open-source T12-ready graph product or vendor dataset was found in these official sources. Diffbot does publish MIT API client libraries, but they wrap its commercial service and do not provide a graph-building implementation or license to Knowledge Graph data. Altana's description of some open-source *inputs* likewise does not license its assembled platform or data. For this spike, use only repository provisional synthetic fixtures and deterministic local code; no vendor integration or dependency is added.

## T12 implications to test

These are experiment recommendations inferred from the product/documentation patterns and the Framework v1 constraints; they are not claims that any vendor implements the exact rules below.

1. Build a projection only from the supplied, versioned statements and explicit ontology. Every edge retains statement/source references, assertion status, valid-time interval, and freshness/projection metadata.
2. Exact identifiers or explicit, reviewed aliases may resolve to one node. Name/product similarity may produce a candidate for review but must not create or merge a business relationship.
3. A direct assertion, a negative assertion, a disputed assertion, and an inferred edge remain distinct. An inferred edge must carry its method and input references; inference does not turn it into a confirmed fact.
4. Absence of an edge means unknown. A path query may report no path within the supplied projection and query bounds, but must not report that the dependency is disproved.
5. Keep valid time separate from observation/projection time so historical queries and stale graph warnings are testable. Rebuilds should be deterministic; an unrelated statement change should not alter unrelated projected edges.
6. Test bounded deterministic traversal over explicit edges, with filters for ontology, relation status, valid time, and historical/current projection. Any alternate ontology is explicit input, not hidden semantic expansion.
7. Model publish as an isolated revisioned operation over a candidate projection. It must not mutate source statements or introduce a second production graph store.

## Source notes and limits

- Altana's current help/legal pages describe graph contents, data streams, an inference layer, subscription access, and restricted use. Marketing claims such as “world's largest” and customer privacy assertions were not independently verified.
- Interos's current mapping page and August 2026 MSA describe the product and access model. Supplier coverage is product/contract-dependent; no account, API, or data access was requested.
- Diffbot docs are explicit about origin and crawl metadata. Its MIT API clients are open-source wrappers, not the KG itself. Confidence semantics describe Diffbot metadata rather than a validated universal probability; its terms disclaim accuracy/completeness.
- Resilinc's 2024 product sheet and indexed 2026 legal page describe the mapping product and service restrictions. Direct retrieval of the current product/legal pages returned 403 during this review. Product-specific rights and the executed Order were not checked; advertised numbers/accuracy remain vendor claims, not a benchmark.
- This is a desk review of public primary sources as of the date above. Product pages and agreements can change. Before any live integration, re-check the executed terms, data-processing terms, allowed use, source licensing, retention, and egress policy.
